# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are recorded here. During 0.x, tool names, parameters and
outputs may change between minor versions; such changes are listed under **Changed**.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-30

First public release.

### Added

- Tools for company search and profiles, filing lists and the filing calendar.
- Reported financials.
- Filing document lists and verbatim document text, and 10-K/10-Q section text.
- Form 13-F manager search, submissions and the institutional holders of a security.
- Form 4 filings, filing detail and insider-trade clusters.
- ETF holdings.
- Watchlist management for your own account.
